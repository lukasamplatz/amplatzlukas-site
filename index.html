Good to know

Questions.

What printer do I need for the file? +

A print bed around 250 mm or larger is ideal. The wall is split into 16 separate teeth, each printed on its own, so you don't need one huge machine — you just need the time (~175 hours total across all parts) and roughly 15 kg of PLA.

Can I print copies and sell them? +

The €196 licence covers printing one wall for your own clinic or space. If you're a lab or studio that wants to print and sell the piece to others, message me for a commercial licence.

How is the finished set shipped? +

Flat-packed — the teeth ship as individual pieces with the mounting template, screws, adhesive and printed instructions. You mount them on-site against the template. Shipping is calculated by destination; for freight-heavy or non-EU destinations I may follow up with an exact quote.

How long until the finished set arrives? +

It's made to order and printing alone runs ~175 hours, so plan for around two months (about 8 weeks) from order to delivery. You'll get a confirmation the moment you order, and an update when it ships.

Do I need special tools to mount it? +

A drill and the included screws and adhesive. The full-scale template shows exactly where each tooth goes, so alignment is straightforward. Budget an afternoon.
